WHO WE ARE

Built from humble beginnings. Grown through trust, hard work and a genuine passion for people.

Step In Recruitment began in 2023 from a small corner of our family home, with little more than a pen, paper and a vision to create a better way for businesses to find great people.

What started small has grown into a trusted recruitment and workforce partner, now operating from our Blacktown headquarters and supporting some of Australia’s leading businesses across warehousing, logistics, manufacturing, distribution, corporate and customer service, retail and e-commerce, hospitality, and events management industries.

Our growth has been driven by one simple belief: recruitment should be about more than filling a vacancy. It should be about understanding a business, its people, its pressures and what it truly needs to succeed.

We’ve built Step In by staying close to our clients, understanding their operations and delivering people they can rely on, whether that means supporting a growing workforce, responding to changing operational demands or finding the right person for a critical role.

While our business has grown, the values we started with haven’t changed. We remain hands-on, people-focused and committed to building genuine partnerships with the businesses and candidates we represent.

People. Partnership. Performance.

We don’t just fill roles. We step into your business, understand what you need, and help you build the workforce to move forward.

OUR MISSION

When businesses need people, we step in.

Our mission is to take the pressure out of building and managing a workforce. We get close enough to our client’s operations to understand what a great hire actually looks like, beyond a resume, a job title or a headcount number.

We step into the busy periods, the growth phases, the hard to fill shifts and the moments when getting the right people matters most.

Because our job isn’t simply to send people to work.

It’s to make sure work gets done.

OUR vision

To change what businesses expect from a recruitment partner.

We’re building a company that sits closer to the operation than the traditional recruitment model, one that understands the floor, the workforce and the commercial reality behind every hiring decision.

Our vision is for Step In to become the name businesses think of when they need a partner who understands their world, moves when they move and grows alongside them.

Not an agency on the outside. A partner that steps in.
